Privacy Policy

How Tera Wallet handles information when you use the website, wallet interface, mobile preview, and related services.

Summary: Tera is designed so wallet keys stay on your device. We aim to send only the information needed to provide a requested service or prepare a proposal.

Effective date: September 17, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to Tera Wallet’s website, dashboard, Android preview, assistant service, bridge and related services that link to this page.

2. Information used by the wallet

Wallet recovery phrases and private keys are created, imported and used locally on your device. Tera does not ask you to send a recovery phrase or private key to our services.

When you connect a wallet or request a proposal, the service may process the public wallet address, the message you submit, the requested action, and technical information needed to operate the request. A bridge request also requires the source and destination addresses, selected assets, amount and route parameters.

3. Assistant and proposal data

The assistant receives the message you choose to submit and, when needed to prepare a proposal, the wallet address. Proposals and local history can be stored in encrypted browser storage when you enable the encrypted vault. You control local retention and can delete conversations, drafts and related metadata from the app.

4. Service providers and networks

Requests may be sent to blockchain RPC providers, asset or eligibility services, quote and bridge providers, and an AI provider when you use the assistant. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices. Public blockchain transactions and addresses are visible on the relevant network by design.

5. Security and logs

We use request identifiers and privacy-preserving operational logs to diagnose service issues. Application logs are designed to hash or redact addresses, amounts, prompts and calldata where practical. No system can guarantee that public blockchain data is private.

6. Your choices

  • Do not submit recovery phrases or private keys through the assistant or any form.
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time.
  • Use the app’s retention controls to remove locally stored assistant data and drafts.
  • Review destination addresses, amounts and transaction details before signing.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact terawalletrh@outlook.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. We will post the revised version here with its effective date.

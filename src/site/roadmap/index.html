Roadmap / Delivery sequence

Build trust.
Then scale authority.

Tera ships custody and review first. Automation, distribution and token utility follow only after the core wallet is ready.

01NOW

Android wallet MVP

A native self-custodial wallet for Android: create or import a recovery phrase, sign locally, review actions, and use core Tera workflows.

  • Preview APK delivery
  • Local recovery and signing
02NEXT

Wallet browser extension

Bring Tera’s approval boundary to browser-native workflows, with the same owner review and policy checks across desktop use cases.

  • Extension signing surface
  • Desktop proposal review
03GATE

Independent security audit

Audit the wallet boundary, key handling, transaction review, and release process before broader distribution.

  • Findings remediation
  • Release readiness review
04SHIP

Play Store and App Store

After the audit gate, prepare store-ready Android and iOS releases with a stable signing and support process.

  • Google Play release
  • App Store release
05TOKEN

200 million $TERA supply burn

Plan and execute a 200,000,000 $TERA supply burn with a public on-chain record, clear accounting, and the required legal and technical review.

  • Published transaction record
  • Transparent supply accounting
06REWARDS

Staking and rewards

Introduce staking and rewards after their security model, token utility, eligibility, and operating rules are defined and reviewed.

  • Staking design
  • Rewards rules and disclosures
07SCALE

Institutional raise and team disclosure

Pursue an institutional fundraise to support audited distribution and operations, then publicly introduce the team behind Tera Wallet.

  • Institutional fundraising process
  • Public team introduction

Roadmap items are delivery intentions, not guarantees. Timing depends on security review, technical readiness, funding and applicable requirements.

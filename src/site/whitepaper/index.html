Private authority for onchain work.

Tera lets an agent prepare real-world asset actions while the owner keeps the final say. Every action is checked, visible, and signed by the person who controls the wallet.

Version 1.0September 2026Robinhood Chain
01 / PROPOSAL

The agent proposes.

Natural-language intent becomes a structured proposal with an action, asset, recipient, amount, and policy context.

02 / REVIEW

You see the checks.

Asset, eligibility, policy, risk, and owner approval gates are shown before a wallet signature is requested.

03 / AUTHORITY

You sign the action.

The agent cannot move funds by itself. The connected owner wallet signs the exact transaction that passed review.

04 / RECEIPT

Results stay traceable.

Pending and completed transactions remain available in receipts, with explorer links and execution status.

How it works

Tera separates preparation from authorization. This makes an assistant useful for research and execution planning without turning it into a custodian.

01Ask the assistant for a transfer, trade, or workflow.
02Tera turns the request into a reviewable proposal.
03Checks run against the asset registry and private policy.
04The owner approves in the connected wallet.
05The signed transaction is submitted and tracked.

Design principles

Owner supervised. Automation prepares; the owner authorizes.

Explicit execution. A signature covers one exact transaction, with recipient, amount, chain, and calldata visible to the wallet.

Policy first. Private rules can shape what an agent may propose and what requires attention.

Inspectable by default. Proposals, approvals, and receipts form a readable trail for every workflow.
